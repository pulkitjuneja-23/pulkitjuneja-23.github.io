Current

  1. PhD project · manuscript in preparation

    Problem
    Spectral information saturates in dense canopies, and models built on it lose accuracy when transferred to a new location.
    Approach
    Integrate UAV-based LiDAR and hyperspectral information to evaluate their combined accuracy and model performance across locations.
    Result
    Fusion was the most accurate configuration within fields (R² = 0.75, RMSE = 0.41 m² m⁻²), while LiDAR alone transferred better across fields.

  2. Extension tool · farmwth.com

    Problem
    On-farm decisions need historical trends alongside real-time data, yet historical records sit in complex formats and need processing before any trend becomes clear.
    Approach
    Developed a tool (farmwth.com) that gives farmers and extension agents historical weather for their site in an easy-to-compare form.
    Result
    30 years of daily weather, 1996 to present, drawn from gridMET, NASA POWER, NOAA/NWS, USDA NASS and Open ET, and served as a season tracker, similar-year comparisons, a next-month outlook and year-by-year views. Used by researchers and extension agents across about 20 counties, with more than 200 visitors a week.

  4. PhD project · ongoing · CANVAS (ASA-CSSA-SSSA) 2025 talk

    Problem
    The fallow period between successive crops leaves soil prone to deterioration, and winter crops grown in the southern United States struggle to produce enough biomass to cover it.
    Approach
    A field trial of a perennial ground cover with different nitrogen rates, tracking biomass, nitrogen credits, erosion and the yield of the following corn crop.
    Result
    Crimson clover and fallow plots generally produced higher corn yield than white clover, across nitrogen rates from 0 to 240 kg ha⁻¹.

    Problem

  2. MS work

    Problem
    Cover crops and residue change how much nitrous oxide, methane and carbon dioxide leave the soil, and those fluxes decide whether a practice is climate-smart.
    Approach
    Measure greenhouse gas fluxes through the season with static chambers across cover crop and residue management treatments, and analyse them by gas chromatography.
    Result
    Carbon dioxide emissions rose through the corn growing season, and cover crop plots emitted more than the control during the fallow period.
    Instruments
    • Static chambers
    • Trace 1300 gas chromatograph
    Statistics
    • lme4
    • emmeans
    • agricolae
    • ggplot2
    Field work
    • Field experiment setup

  3. MS thesis work · manuscript accepted

    Problem
    Warm-season cover crops use water between cash crops, which matters in Central Texas where the following corn crop depends on stored soil moisture.
    Approach
    Two years of field trials evaluating the feasibility of warm-season cover crops following corn, and their impact on corn yield and water use efficiency.
    Result
    Sunn hemp plots produced higher corn grain and biomass than sorghum–sudangrass. Both cover crops carried minimal penalties in the tradeoff analysis, with no difference in evapotranspiration.

    • Stevens soil moisture sensors
    • Geoprobe MC5
